ABOUT THE PROJECT

Giving words a deeper meaning.

EMOTIX is designed to analyze written text and determine the emotion expressed by the user. The system focuses on five major emotions: Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, and Neutral.

By applying Natural Language Processing, EMOTIX demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can be used to understand and classify human emotions through text.

THE PROCESS

How EMOTIX works

A simple three-step process for analyzing emotions from written text.

01

Enter Text

The user provides a sentence or message that they want the system to analyze.

02

Process with NLP

Natural Language Processing is used to analyze the words and context of the input.

03

Detect Emotion

The system identifies the most likely emotion expressed in the text.

EMOTION CLASSIFICATION

Understanding the results

Emotion Description Sample Expression
Joy Expresses happiness or excitement. "I'm really happy today!"
Sadness Expresses sorrow or disappointment. "I feel sad about what happened."
Anger Expresses frustration or irritation. "That situation made me angry."
Fear Expresses worry or nervousness. "I'm nervous about tomorrow."
Neutral Shows no strong emotional expression. "The meeting starts at eight."
